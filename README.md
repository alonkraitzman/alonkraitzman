# Alon P. Kraitzman, PhD

Research Scientist & Senior Consultant at Gallup, based in Sydney. My work spans employee and customer experience, organisational culture, public opinion and behavioural science.

I design and lead quantitative, qualitative and mixed-methods research, from framing questions and developing the approach to analysis and interpretation.

- **[Singapore Workplace Report 2026](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/711230/singapore-workplace-report-2026.aspx)** · Gallup and Singapore Institute of Directors, 2026 · **Research and writing**.
